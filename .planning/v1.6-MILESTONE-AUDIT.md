`ProposalRow`, whose row handler (`ProposalRow.tsx:136`) navigates to `/proposals/{id}`.

with no admin bypass (`app/(authed)/proposals/[id]/page.tsx:47-48`), so an admin 404s.

relationship-level summary (LC ref, amount, date, status, counts) is visible to admins,
so CRM-03's core promise holds. Already logged in `deferred-items.md` with a resolution
path and deferred to Phase 33/34; tracked here as a **warning**, not a new blocker.

## Assessment

Nothing shipped in v1.6 so far is broken, and the two properties the milestone is *built
around* — CRM-02 tenant isolation and the CRM-05 snapshot invariant — are both verified
against real behaviour rather than asserted. The milestone is not close to done, but its
foundation is sound.

Three things stand between here and a milestone that could be audited as passed:

1. **Four phases have not been started** (31–34, carrying 17 of 31 requirements). This is
   the dominant gap and it is schedule, not defect.
2. **Neither executed phase was verified.** The evidence exists; the artifact does not.
   Running `/gsd-verify-work` on 29 and 30 would convert 14 requirements from `partial`
   to `satisfied` without any new code.
3. **INFRA-05 rests on inference.** Worth an explicit decision to accept, since the probe
   was deliberately skipped rather than forgotten.
