<objective>
Prove the swap actually worked, in the two ways nothing in the repo currently catches: that every
character the document renders in French and English resolves in the registered Inter faces (D-09),
and that four genuinely distinct faces are embedded rather than one face reused four times (D-10).
Then run the full gate and hand the result to Antoine for a visual pass (D-11).

D-10 exists because weight fidelity is the failure mode with no natural alarm: tofu is visible and a
missing file throws, but "all four weights rendered at 400" produces a valid, gate-green, subtly wrong
document — exactly what a variable-font registration would have produced.

Purpose: automated glyph coverage cannot see "technically renders, looks wrong", and a human eye
cannot see "these four subsets are byte-identical". Both gates are needed, and the phase closes on
the human one.
Output: one new git-tracked test file, a green full gate, and a recorded human approval.
</objective>
