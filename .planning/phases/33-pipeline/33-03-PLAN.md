## A-2 resolved — the conversion-rate denominator

33-UI-SPEC.md A-2 leaves the formula to the planner. Locked here:

- **Numerator** — the caller's proposals with `outcome = 'won'`.
- **Denominator** — the caller's proposals that are `status = 'active'`, `deleted_at IS NULL`, and
  `client_relationship_id IS NOT NULL`.

Why that denominator and not `won + lost`: PIPE-03 asks for a *per-quote* conversion rate, and D-06's
whole rationale is that the rate must "stay honest for partners who never tidy up, rather than
silently measuring only the ones who do their admin". A `won + lost` denominator would exclude every
un-triaged quote and flatter the inattentive. Drafts are excluded because they are not quotes yet;
soft-deleted rows are excluded because they are withdrawn; `client_relationship_id IS NULL` rows are
excluded because they have no UI path to ever receive an outcome, so including them would create a
permanently unimprovable drag on the number.

- **Zero denominator** renders as `—`, not `0 %`. A 0% rate is a meaningful claim ("you convert
  nothing"); an undefined rate is not the same statement. UIC-08's render-real-zeros rule applies to
  counts, and the sublabel still renders `0 gagnée(s) sur 0 proposition(s)` literally.
- **Rounding** — integer percent, `Math.round(won / total * 100)`.
</decision_record>
