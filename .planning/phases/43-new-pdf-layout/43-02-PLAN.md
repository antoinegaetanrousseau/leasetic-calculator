<objective>
Port the two brand marks to `@react-pdf/renderer`'s `<Svg>` primitives, evidence-first (D-07).

Purpose: `document.tsx` has zero `<Svg>` or `<Image>` usage today — this is greenfield, and D-07
names a failure mode with no natural alarm. Two of the four ellipses in both source SVGs carry
`transform="rotate(-90 cx cy)"`; react-pdf's partial SVG support may drop that transform *silently*,
drawing a tall ellipse where a wide one belongs. This plan lands the marks and proves they rendered
before any layout work depends on the header's height.

Output: `LeaseticLockup` and `LeaseticIcon` components plus an evidence test that decides D-07's
question by inspecting real rendered bytes, not by assumption.

**The deliverable does not branch on the verdict.** Both marks are authored with the rotation
pre-applied at authoring time (D-07's option 1), which is geometrically exact and therefore correct
whether or not react-pdf honours `rotate(-90 cx cy)`. A 90° rotation of an axis-aligned ellipse about
its own centre is exactly a swap of `rx` and `ry`, so the transform is eliminated rather than
depended on. The evidence test exists to *record* what react-pdf actually does with the transform,
and to catch the residual case where even the untransformed ellipses fail to draw — which is the only
condition that would escalate to D-07's option 2 (a committed 3× PNG via `<Image>`).
</objective>
