   `<details>` bullets, and only the current milestone's own phases get full `## Phase Details`.
   D-40-05 requires the v1.1 shape. Re-scoping v1.7's pair is explicitly out of scope.

2. **The known-gaps section comes from the audit, not from the planner.** D-40-02 feeds plan
   40-03's freshly-measured findings into the entry. This plan does not derive, adjust or soften
   them — it transcribes.

Purpose: CLOSE-06 requires the entry plus both snapshots. This is the last piece of v1.6's record
before plan 40-06 moves its phase directories to where the entry says they are.
Output: a v1.6 entry in `MILESTONES.md`, a new `milestones/v1.6-ROADMAP.md`, and an archive header
on `milestones/v1.6-REQUIREMENTS.md`.
