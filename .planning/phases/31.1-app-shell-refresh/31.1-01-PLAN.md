<objective>
Replace the `--radius × multiplier` mechanism (`app/globals.css:481-487`) with an explicit,
per-step radius scale, and introduce the named container-radius token this phase exists to
create (D-01, D-02, D-03). Also lands the one other pure-value shell dimension edit in this
file: `--topbar-h` 64px → 52px.

Purpose: `app/globals.css:66-74` records the incident this plan is re-approaching — `--radius`
was briefly `1rem`, every step derived from it via a multiplier, so `rounded-4xl` hit 41.6px and
every `Input`/`InputGroup` rendered as a pill. UIC-04 exists because of that. D-02 supersedes
the multiplier mechanism so the regression becomes **structurally impossible**, not merely
currently-avoided.

Output: an explicit radius scale, a `--radius-container` ladder at Colibris's measured
12/16/24/32/40 values, and a permanent automated gate that fails if anyone re-couples the scale
or re-points the control tier.

## READ THIS BEFORE EDITING — three verified corrections to `31.1-UI-SPEC.md`

The UI-SPEC's Radius Scale section rests on two claims that are **false in this repo**. Both
were verified by direct grep on 2026-09-02. Do not "restore" the spec's version.

**Correction 1 — controls do NOT read `--radius`.** The UI-SPEC says "Retain `--radius:
0.625rem` only as the value shadcn's own control primitives (`Input`, `Button`, `Select`,
`Badge`) read directly." They do not. `grep -rn "var(--radius)" app src` returns **only** the
seven multiplier lines at `app/globals.css:481-487` — nothing else in the repo reads
`var(--radius)`. base-maia's control primitives use the Tailwind utility **`rounded-4xl`**,
which resolves to `--radius-4xl` = `calc(var(--radius) * 2.6)` = **26px**:
`src/components/ui/button.tsx:7`, `input.tsx:12`, `select.tsx:45`, `badge.tsx:8`, plus
`toggle.tsx`, `toggle-group.tsx`, `input-group.tsx`, `tabs.tsx`, `tooltip.tsx`, `progress.tsx`,
`button-group.tsx`, `combobox.tsx`, `dialog.tsx:57`, `alert-dialog.tsx:53`,
`src/components/reui/badge.tsx`, `src/components/reui/cascader/cascader.tsx` — 16 non-vendored
files.

**Correction 2 — `--radius-3xl` and `--radius-4xl` cannot be retired.** UI-SPEC Assumption A-4
assumes no external call site. There are two classes of consumer:
`src/components/ui/calendar.tsx:35` reads `var(--radius-4xl)` for `[--cell-radius:...]`, and the
16 files above consume it through the `rounded-4xl` utility. `rounded-3xl` is consumed by
`src/components/ui/toggle-group.tsx:75` (4 occurrences). **Deleting either declaration makes
Tailwind fall back to its own default `--radius-4xl: 2rem` (32px), which would turn every
Button, Input, Select and Badge into a full pill — a verbatim re-run of the UIC-04 incident and
a direct violation of ROADMAP criterion 4.**

**Correction 3 — ROADMAP criterion 4's "8px" is a description of Colibris, not of Leasétic.**
Leasétic's control tier resolves to 26px today (clamped by the browser to half the control
height on short controls). The binding half of criterion 4 is "**keeps its current** corner."
This plan keeps the current value exactly and records the real number; it does **not** move
controls to 8px, because that would be an unrequested visual change to every control in the app.

## The resolution (uses the discretion CONTEXT.md granted)

`31.1-CONTEXT.md` § Claude's Discretion explicitly delegates "**whether the explicit radius
scale keeps the `--radius-*` naming or introduces new names**, and how `--radius` itself is
retained or retired." This plan uses that delegation:

- The existing `--radius-sm … --radius-4xl` namespace is **frozen at its current computed pixel
  values, declared explicitly** — the multiplier mechanism is gone (D-02 satisfied), and nothing
  in the app changes appearance on day one (D-03 and criterion 4 satisfied).
- Colibris's measured 12/16/24/32/40 ladder is declared under its **own** names
  (`--radius-container-*`), with `--radius-container` as the semantic role token (D-01, D-02).
- Containers therefore read a token that **no control primitive reads**, which is the structural
  property criterion 4 is asking for: the top of the container ladder physically cannot reach
  the control tier, because they share no variable.
</objective>
