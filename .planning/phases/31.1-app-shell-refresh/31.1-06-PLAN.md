## Two verified corrections to `31.1-UI-SPEC.md` § Sidebar — read before writing the badge

**Correction 1 — the UI-SPEC's badge would render an invisible logo.** Its snippet puts the mark
inside `bg-sidebar-primary`. `public/logo-mark.svg` is four ellipses, **all** filled `#01CC72`,
and `--sidebar-primary` resolves to `var(--brand-accent)` = `#01cc72` in both themes. That is
green on green — a blank accent square where the mark should be. Use a neutral badge fill
(`bg-sidebar-accent`) and keep the mark its own green, which is also what ships today (the mark
currently sits directly on the sidebar surface). Do **not** mint a white logo variant as part of
this plan; if the operator later wants an accent badge, that is an asset task with its own scope.

**Correction 2 — symmetric padding does not centre these children.** The UI-SPEC argues that
because `P + (W − 2P)/2 = W/2`, a 12px inset and an 8px inset centre on the same axis. That
identity holds only for a child that fills its content box. Both children here are fixed 32px
wide inside a `justify-content: flex-start` row, so they sit at the **left edge** of their content
box: in a 68px rail, `SidebarHeader`'s `p-3` puts the badge's centre at 12 + 16 = 28px, and
`SidebarGroup`'s `p-2` puts a `size-8` menu button's centre at 8 + 16 = 24px. Neither is the
rail's 34px centre, and they are 4px apart from each other. Symmetric padding is necessary and not
sufficient. The fix below centres each child **within** its box, which makes both land on 34px
regardless of the differing insets — that is the "by construction" property D-09 asks for, and it
means `AppSidebar.tsx:181`'s `p-3` does **not** need normalising to `p-2` (leave it; changing it
would move the expanded header's inset by 4px, which D-09 explicitly puts out of scope).
</objective>
