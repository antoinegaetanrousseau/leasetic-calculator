## Accomplishments

- `UI-CONVENTIONS.md`'s UIC-04 no longer contradicts the shipped code: it names the control tier
  (26px, `--radius-4xl`) and the container tier (16px, `--radius-container`) as structurally
  disjoint namespaces, states `--radius: 0.625rem` drives nothing, and names
  `tests/radius-scale.test.ts` / `tests/container-radius.test.ts` as the re-verification
  instruction instead of a manual form check.
- OPEN-A (container radius split) and OPEN-C (deferred app-shell directives) are both closed with
  their historical reasoning kept intact for provenance, including which two `app-shell-1`
  mechanics were deliberately **not** adopted (className-set sidebar widths, the vendored
  `SidebarRailToggle`'s English-only label).
- UIC-03 carries a dark-mode addendum: accent unchanged (`#01cc72`, zero `#009999` teal), four
  sampled shell-surface roles with their values, three explicitly unsampled gaps with a closure
  path, the collapsed-badge `bg-sidebar-accent` finding, and the shell-dimension note (252/68px
  sidebar, 52px header, 120px lockup, inline-`style` requirement).
- The operator walked all ten checkpoint items against the running app and signed off; `CLAUDE.md`
  was updated with the approved replacement text for its Open Items section.

## Checkpoint Verdict (Task 3)

The operator resolved the `checkpoint:human-verify` gate. Recorded verdict and evidence:

1. **Control corner (Criterion 4).** `--radius-4xl` = 26px live; the shadcn `Input` primitive is
   unchanged (`rounded-4xl`). The login page's 12px inputs are a pre-existing legacy surface
   (`LoginForm.tsx` uses raw inputs on legacy classes, not the shadcn `Input` primitive) — this
   phase changed no legacy form rule. Every `input`/`form` hit in the `globals.css` diff is comment
   prose; the only changed declarations are `--radius-*`, `--radius-container-*`, and four dark
   tokens.
2. **Container radius (Criteria 4/5).** `--radius-container` = 16px live; sample card renders 16px.
3. **Collapsed rail (Criterion 1).** Mark badge + nav icons only — no chevron, no vertical stack —
   and `bg-sidebar-accent` keeps the green mark visible against the badge fill.
4. **D-09 centring (Criterion 1).** Rail 67px, centre 33.5px. Both the badge centre and the
   nav-icon centre measured at **33.5px** — exact axis match. For reference, Colibris's own badge
   sits at 26.0 against its icon centre of 33.5, so Leasétic's collapsed rail is measurably better
   centred than the reference it was aligned to.
5. **Control uniqueness (Criterion 2).** Exactly one control announces a toggle name:
   `data-sidebar="trigger"`, `tabIndex=0`, `aria-label="Basculer la barre latérale"` (French UI,
   `html lang=fr`). `SidebarRail` keeps a hardcoded English label but is `aria-hidden="true"` /
   `tabIndex=-1`, excluded from the accessibility tree.
6. **Breadcrumb (Criterion 3).** Renders in-language; "Accueil" on the root route.
7. **Proportions (Criterion 6).** Sidebar 251px (subpixel of the specified 252px), lockup 120px,
   eyebrow 11px / 0.55px letter-spacing — matches Colibris exactly.
8. **Header (Criterion 6).** `--topbar-h` = 52px; header renders 52px.
9. **Dark mode (Criterion 7) — PARTIAL.** The token layer is verified: six tokens carry screenshot
   provenance, zero `#009999` teal exists anywhere, and print/PDF-white plus no-flash restoration
   are pinned by `tests/dark-palette.test.ts`. **The live visual pass and the PDF-white
   confirmation were NOT performed** — the browser session dropped mid-check. This is recorded
   honestly as a partial verification, not claimed as complete.
10. **Dark-mode gaps decision (item 9 of the checkpoint script).** `DARK-GAP-01` (dialog/popover
    elevation), `DARK-GAP-02` (input fill), `DARK-GAP-03` (sidebar hover/active) are **accepted
    as-is** at their pre-existing values. No second screenshot was supplied. No value was invented
    to close them — they remain pinned by `tests/dark-palette.test.ts`. Filling one later requires
    a new measurement plus a deliberate test edit.
11. **`CLAUDE.md` approval (item 10 of the checkpoint script).** Approved verbatim. The stale
    claims that OPEN-A is deferred to this phase and that `--radius`/the `@theme inline`
    multipliers are pinned were replaced with text stating `--radius` drives nothing in the scale
    and that OPEN-A closed in this phase.
