<open_question_resolutions>
**OQ-5 — Contact conflict across provenance: the extraction never touches a contact it did not
create.** Decided here; record the reasoning in the SUMMARY.

Concretely: when a planned contact matches an existing contact in the target relationship (by
email, else by normalized name, per D-06) and that existing row has `source IS NULL` — meaning a
partner typed it in by hand — the extraction writes **nothing**: no update, no second contact
row. The collision is reported as a `contact_conflicts_with_human_row` skip (plan 02 classifies
it; this module honours the classification and never re-derives it).

Why not merge into it: D-06's fill-blanks-never-overwrite rule is safe among machine-created
rows, where the worst case is that the engine corrects its own earlier guess. Writing into a
partner's hand-curated row is a different act entirely — it is exactly the un-undoable bulk edit
D-08's provenance marker exists to make impossible, and CRM-04 frames a contact's details as the
owning partner's own asset. Why not create a duplicate: that pollutes the partner's client book
with a near-identical person for no gain. Reporting it and leaving it alone is the only option
that neither destroys nor litters.

When the matching existing contact has `source = 'proposal_extraction'`, the fill-blanks update
proceeds normally — the engine is allowed to correct itself.
</open_question_resolutions>
