<objective>
Archive phases 28-35 into their milestone directories by hand, repair the three test suites that
hardcode the pre-move layout, and tick CLOSE-06 and CLOSE-07.

**The GSD archiving CLI is not used.** D-40-08 is a deliberate refusal, traceable to the retraction
printed in v1.7's own MILESTONES.md entry: the CLI "attributed every phase on disk to this
milestone", producing the wrong 19 phases / 97 plans / 178 tasks figures that entry had to retract.
Phases move by hand `git mv` against an explicit phase→milestone map written first.

**The move breaks tests, and fixing them ships here, not in a follow-up.** Three suites assert the
current layout by literal path. `tests/_planning-docs.ts`'s `resolvePhaseDoc()` was written
specifically for CLOSE-07 and is archive-resilient — but its **own** suite, plus two others,
hardcode `.planning/phases/…` as the expected answer. They go red the moment the move lands, not
because a requirement regressed but because the documents moved. That resolver's found-in-**both**
throw is also a free correctness check on this plan: if a `git mv` leaves a copy behind, the suite
goes red for the right reason.

the phases a current or future milestone still needs — 36 through 40.
Output: 8 phase directories relocated, 3 test files repaired, live path references swept, CLOSE-06
and CLOSE-07 closed.
