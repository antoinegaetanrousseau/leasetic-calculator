<decision_record>
## `client_relationships.source` is ALREADY TAKEN — the FICHE-04 field is `lead_source`

The design (§1) and 34-CONTEXT `<specifics>` both say `client_relationships` gains a `source`
column CHECK-constrained to `recommandation | prospection | salon | site_web | autre`.

**That column already exists and means something else.** `src/db/schema.ts:433` declares
`source: text('source')` on `clientRelationships` as the Phase 31 D-08 **provenance marker** —
"NULL means entered by a human" — and `client_relationships_source_check` restricts it to
`NULL | 'proposal_extraction' | 'hubspot_import'`. Its documented purpose is to let a bad bulk
import be surgically undone by deleting every row carrying its source value. `companies.source`
carries the identical contract at `src/db/schema.ts:407`.

Widening `client_relationships_source_check` to admit the five lead-source values would fuse two
unrelated vocabularies into one column: `--remove` on a fixture seeder, the Phase 31 undo path,
and any future provenance query would all start matching partner-entered lead sources. That is a
data-integrity regression, not a naming inconvenience.

**Chosen: a new column `lead_source`, with its own CHECK
`client_relationships_lead_source_check`.** The Phase 31 provenance column is untouched, its CHECK
is untouched, and the two vocabularies never meet. Everything downstream in this phase — the
schema, the dialog, the dictionary keys, the audit payload — uses `lead_source` / `leadSource`,
and the phase's FICHE-04 requirement text ("source") maps onto it.

An acceptance criterion below asserts `git diff src/db/schema.ts` produces zero changed lines
mentioning `client_relationships_source_check` or `companies_source_check`, so this cannot drift
back during implementation.

## `naf_label` does not exist, and no `naf_label` column is created

D-06 removed it after the API was measured: the registry returns codes with no `libelle` anywhere
in the payload. `naf_code` stores the raw code; `naf_section` stores the one-letter section, whose
21-entry label table plan 34-02 ships in code. An acceptance criterion greps for `naf_label` and
requires zero matches, because it was present in the first draft of the design and is the single
most likely thing to be reintroduced from memory.
</decision_record>
