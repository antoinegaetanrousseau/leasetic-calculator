# Phase 31.1 Plan 01: Radius Scale Decoupling Summary

**Replaced the `--radius × multiplier` mechanism with seven frozen px declarations plus a separate Colibris-measured `--radius-container` ladder, closing OPEN-A and adding a permanent 7-assertion regression gate against the original pill incident.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~9 min
- **Started:** 2026-09-02T21:43:00+02:00 (approx, first edit)
- **Completed:** 2026-09-02T21:49:01+02:00
- **Tasks:** 3/3
- **Files modified:** 2 (1 modified, 1 created)

## Accomplishments

- `app/globals.css`'s `@theme inline` block no longer contains any `calc(var(--radius) * n)` expression — the seven control-tier steps (`--radius-sm` through `--radius-4xl`) are now standalone `px` declarations, structurally immune to the "raise `--radius`, every step inflates" failure mode that produced the original `rounded-4xl` = 41.6px pill incident.
- Added the Colibris-measured `--radius-container-xs|sm|md|lg|xl` ladder (12/16/24/32/40px) plus `--radius-container` (= 16px, D-01) under its own namespace — containers and controls now share zero variables.
- `.card` moved from `--radius-2xl` (18px) to `--radius-container` (16px), closing `OPEN-A` in `UI-CONVENTIONS.md`.
- `--topbar-h` dropped from 64px to 52px at its single declaration site; `Topbar.tsx`'s `h-[var(--topbar-h)]` and the proposal detail page's sticky offset follow automatically, no literal introduced anywhere.
- `tests/radius-scale.test.ts` — a new 7-assertion source-only test suite that fails the build if: the scale is re-coupled to `--radius`, the control tier moves off 22/26px, a control primitive stops reaching `rounded-4xl`, the calendar's `--cell-radius` stops resolving, the container ladder stops being separate from the control tier, `.card` drifts off `--radius-container`, or `--topbar-h` stops being 52px through the variable.

## Decisions Made

- The existing `--radius-*` namespace keeps its names (Discretion, `31.1-CONTEXT.md`), frozen at current pixel values rather than renamed or retired — zero-visual-change day one.
- The container ladder is a **new, separate** namespace (`--radius-container*`) rather than reusing `--radius-sm...2xl` — this is the structural property ROADMAP criterion 4 actually asks for (containers can never reach the control tier again because they share no variable), and avoids silently moving ~90 unaudited `rounded-2xl`/`rounded-md` call sites.
- `--radius: 0.625rem` stays declared (not deleted): `shadcn add` regeneration expects it, and `src/components/ui/sidebar.tsx` locally re-binds it via `[--radius:var(--radius-xl)]`. Its comment now states the truth — it drives nothing in the scale any more.
- **Real measured control corner: 26px** (`--radius-4xl`), confirmed by the emitted build CSS (`--radius-4xl:26px`) and by direct grep of `button.tsx`/`input.tsx`/`select.tsx`/`badge.tsx` all reaching it via `rounded-4xl`. ROADMAP criterion 4's "8px" describes Colibris, not Leasétic — Plan 31.1-07's UIC-04 rewrite should cite 26px, not 8px, as Leasétic's control-tier value. `--radius-3xl` = 22px is the other load-bearing step (`toggle-group.tsx`'s 4 directional corners).
