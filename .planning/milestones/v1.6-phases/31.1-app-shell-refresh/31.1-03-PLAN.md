## Composition note — the Breadcrumb primitive has no in-app precedent

`31.1-PATTERNS.md` verified by repo-wide grep that **zero** real pages render
`src/components/ui/breadcrumb.tsx` today. `aide/commencer-ici/page.tsx` and
`partners/new/page.tsx` hand-roll `<Link>` with inline styles; they are **not** analogs. The only
compositions in the repo are vendored blocks. Build from the primitive's own API, documented
below from source — do not go looking for an existing call site, and do not import anything under
`src/components/blocks/`.

Primitive contract (`src/components/ui/breadcrumb.tsx`, read it):
- `BreadcrumbLink` is `useRender.ComponentProps<"a">` with `defaultTagName: "a"` — it takes
  `href` directly, and also accepts a `render` element for a custom anchor.
- `BreadcrumbPage` already sets `role="link"`, `aria-disabled="true"` and `aria-current="page"`
  and renders a `<span>`. It is the non-link current page out of the box; add no overrides.
- `BreadcrumbSeparator` renders the divider; it is a sibling of `BreadcrumbItem`, not a child.
</objective>
