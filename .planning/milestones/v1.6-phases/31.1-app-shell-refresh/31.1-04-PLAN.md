<objective>
Back-apply the `--radius-container` token created in Plan 31.1-01 to every container surface, and
retire the three `rounded-[24px]` literals Phase 31 shipped (D-01). This is the second half of
closing OPEN-A.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 requires container surfaces to render at a **named token, never a
per-file literal**, and criterion 5 requires Phase 30's `.card` and Phase 31's review-queue cards
to land on the same radius. Plan 31.1-01 moved `.card` to 16px; without this plan, `.card` (16px)
and shadcn's own `Card` (`rounded-2xl`, 18px) would disagree — which is **exactly** the defect
UIC-04's `.card` clause records ("three metric tiles at the Card radius sat above a section card
at 16px on the home page"). Landing only half of this change re-creates a documented incident.

Output: one container radius across `.card`, shadcn `Card`, `Dialog`, `AlertDialog`, `Popover`
and the three Phase 31 surfaces, plus an automated gate against literal drift.

## Scope line (stated so it is not silently widened or narrowed)

**In:** `.card` (done in 31.1-01), `card.tsx`, `dialog.tsx`, `alert-dialog.tsx`, `popover.tsx`,
`PairReviewCard.tsx`, `MergeDialog.tsx`, `KeepSeparateDialog.tsx`.

**Out, with reasons:**
- Control-attached menu surfaces — `select.tsx`'s content panel, `dropdown-menu`, `combobox`,
  `cascader`. These are anchored to a control and read the control tier by design; moving them
  is a separate visual decision nobody made.
- `card.tsx`'s inner `rounded-t-xl` / `rounded-b-xl` clipping on `CardHeader`/`CardFooter`/
  first-child images. Those clip content against the outer corner and are not container surfaces.
- `src/components/proposals/EmbeddedPdfPreview.tsx:30`'s `rounded-[12px]`. It frames the PDF page
  surface governed by the Phase 5 / Phase 8 print invariant (`app/globals.css:214-218`), not a
  shell container. Leaving it is deliberate; Plan 31.1-07 records it as a named exception rather
  than letting a checker read it as a missed literal.
- Everything under `src/components/blocks/` (vendored reference blocks, never imported).
</objective>
