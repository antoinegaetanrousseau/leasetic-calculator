<recorded_divergence>
**D-16 vs the ratified UI-SPEC — recorded, not silent.**

`31-CONTEXT.md` D-16 says the review queue gets "its **own** admin route, **alongside** the
`/[adminSegment]/companies` tree Phase 30 shipped." The approved and checker-verified
`31-UI-SPEC.md` (§ "Route map", Assumption A-1) places it at
`/[adminSegment]/companies/review` — a sibling of `page.tsx` and `[id]/` *inside* that tree,
rather than a new top-level segment.

**The UI-SPEC is binding** — it is the ratified contract and the checker verified it. This plan
implements `/[adminSegment]/companies/review`. The divergence is recorded here so it is a
decision on the record rather than drift: "alongside the companies tree" is read as a sibling
directory within the tree, on the reasoning the UI-SPEC gives (a flagged pair is fundamentally
about the company registry, not a separate domain), and D-16's substantive requirements — its
own route, its own component tree, its own empty state, its own resolve actions — are all
satisfied. Record this in the SUMMARY.

**Load-bearing consequence:** because the route is nested, `getRouteMeta`'s tail match for
`'/companies/review'` MUST be evaluated **before** the existing `'/companies'` match, or the new
route resolves to the `admin-companies` nav state. This is the same prefix-collision class the
existing `/coefficients` / `/partners` / `/companies` chain already avoids by ordering.
</recorded_divergence>
